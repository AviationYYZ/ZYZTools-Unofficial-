Discord Rich Presence

Uses the shared ZYZTools application (1560430298968105010). Keep this app and Discord desktop open, and enable Discord activity sharing. Enable this in only one of the production/test apps at a time. Your position, traffic summary and configured links are publicly visible on your Discord profile.

Positions ending in _APP or _CTR use all aircraft whose tags you own in EuroScope. Other positions count aircraft near the listed airports using VATSIM's public feed. Each aircraft is counted once, including departures, landed arrivals and local traffic. Ground counts are estimated, not an explicit feed field. VATSIM is refreshed at most once a minute; owned-tag counts refresh every five seconds.

Variables: {position}, {airports}, {count}, {traffic}, {source}. Discord truncates expanded lines to 128 characters. The application name is managed in the Discord Developer Portal.

Ground-count detection

Airport coordinates/elevations are downloaded once from the public mwgg/Airports dataset and cached locally. Adjust these tolerances for your airports. Unknown airports and unavailable/stale feeds clear the status instead of showing zero traffic.

Buttons may not appear when viewing your own activity; check from another Discord account. Clear both fields to remove a button. Disconnecting or closing EuroScope clears the presence within about 20 seconds, including crash recovery.

Command reference and simulation limits
APP CLEAR arms a generic sector-centreline/3° simulated ILS for the runway selected in the flight plan. It does not instantly turn or dive onto final: a suitable intercept vector and altitude are needed. IAS is converted to TAS using approximate ISA density, with no wind. This is not a certified approach/performance model.

            
ALT feet · RATE ft/min · SPD / IAS indicated knots · HDG/LHDG/RHDG degrees magnetic · DCT fix · SQ 1234 · MODE C/STBY · IDENT (18 simulation seconds) · ROUTE RESUME. Scheduled aircraft appear when simulation time reaches their spawn time.
